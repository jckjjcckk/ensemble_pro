# Acceptance: touch on the iPad

Run on a real iPad in Safari, landscape and portrait, on every screen after each change. Mark each line pass or fail with a note. Drafted by Claude 2026-10-27 from the iPad-first rules in PRODUCT.md; CK edits.

Open `http://<mac-ip>:5173` on the same wifi (or the tunnel URL). Fixture: the Beethoven quartet, Hart House Chamber Players.

| # | Screen | Do this | Pass means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Library | Tap each library in the switcher, then tap a cover. | The information box opens beside the grid (landscape) or over it (portrait); Open score, Practise or Order pages are reachable with one more tap. |
| 2 | Library | Tap the display button, choose Bound, then Dark. | Type and colour change; nothing moves. The choice survives a reload. |
| 3 | Score | Swipe left and right over the pages. | The spread turns. A short tap on a bar does not turn the page. |
| 4 | Score | Tap a bar with no tool open. | Nothing happens except any open note closing. No accidental marking. |
| 5 | Score | Press and hold a bar for half a second. | The marking composer opens in the dock with that bar number; no text-selection or callout sheet appears. |
| 6 | Score | Landscape: look for the words beside the page. Portrait: open Markings. | In landscape a voice note or text note sits in the outer margin level with its bar, with a tick at the bar's height; in portrait the Markings panel lists the same notes. |
| 7 | Score | Drag the bracket on the ruler with a finger. | The pages follow the bracket; the dock and the ruler stay put. |
| 8 | Score | Tap a presence face in the header. | The spread jumps to that member's bar. The face is at least 44 pt. |
| 9 | Practice | With one hand, change tempo with minus and plus, toggle Metronome, press Play. | Every control hits on the first try; held minus/plus repeats. |
| 10 | Practice | Drag the loop bracket on the ruler, then tap a heat band. | The loop updates live; the trouble-spot card appears with Loop these bars. |
| 11 | Scan | Drag a page to a new position with a finger. | A floating copy follows the finger and the bracket shows where it lands; the order changes on release. |
| 12 | All | Rotate the iPad. | Landscape shows two facing pages, portrait one; nothing is cropped; the dock remains fully visible. |

Not yet built (do not mark): chrome hide/show on tap with a visible handle in document mode.
